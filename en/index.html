The story of Casa da Lua

Before it was an inn, it was a home

Tati arrived in Alto Paraíso in 2009. The house was finished in 2011 and, at the end of the following year, began to receive guests. It grew into rooms, gardens and meeting spaces without losing the domestic scale that gave it its origin

How it all began

The house grew without losing its feel of home

In December 2012, the residence began to receive guests. New suites came later, then leisure spaces, wellness areas and gatherings

What ties these phases together is a simple way of occupying the place: coffee in the morning, paths through the garden, shade, water, the table and the fire at the end of the day. The structure grew; the feeling of being in a home remained,

Before choosing an outing, it is worth confirming:

It is worth considering children, pets, transport and the real pace of the group before heading out.

    Privacy policy

    How we look after your data.

    Updated on October 20, 2026

    Who we are

    This policy applies to the website of Casa da Lua Pousada, in Alto Paraíso de Goiás (GO), Brazil, and explains how we handle the personal data of people who visit it, ask for information or book a stay.

    What data we handle

    • What you fill in on the forms (name, contact, dates, number of people, type of event and message). The forms build the message and open the inn's WhatsApp: it is only sent when you confirm it in WhatsApp, which has its own policy.
    • Booking data. Bookings are made on the Omnibees booking engine. The guest and payment data entered there are handled by the inn and by Omnibees, under the engine's own policy. This site does not collect or store card data.
    • Browsing data, such as pages visited, type of device and browser, collected through cookies and audience and advertising measurement tools, when they are active.

    What we use it for

    To answer your requests, complete and manage bookings, look after you during your stay, understand how the site is used so we can improve it and, with your consent, send news and dates of upcoming programs.

    Who we share it with

    Only with those needed for the service: Omnibees (bookings), WhatsApp (conversations), the company that hosts the site and the measurement tools we use. We do not sell personal data.

    Cookies

    Cookies store preferences and help measure how the site is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some features may stop working as expected.

    Your rights

    Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law 13,709/2018), you may ask us to confirm that we handle your data, and to give you access to it, correct it, delete it, make it portable and revoke your consent, where applicable.

    How long we keep it

    For as long as needed to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected and the inn's legal obligations. After that, the data is deleted or anonymized.

    Changes

    We may update this policy. The date of the last update is always at the top of this page.

    Get in touch

    Questions, requests or complaints about personal data: contato@casadaluapousada.com.br.
