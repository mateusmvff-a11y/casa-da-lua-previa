58|Who we are
59|This policy applies to the website of Casa da Lua Pousada, in Alto Paraíso de Goiás (GO), Brazil, and explains how we handle the personal data of people who visit it, ask for information or book a stay.
60|What data we handle
61|What you fill in on the forms
62|(name, contact, dates, number of people, type of event and message). The forms build the message and open the inn's WhatsApp: it is only sent when you confirm it in WhatsApp, which has its own policy.
63|Booking data.
64|Bookings are made on the Omnibees booking engine. The guest and payment data entered there are handled by the inn and by Omnibees, under the engine's own policy. This site does not collect or store card data.
65|Browsing data
66|, such as pages visited, type of device and browser, collected through cookies and audience and advertising measurement tools, when they are active.
67|What we use it for
68|To answer your requests, complete and manage bookings, look after you during your stay, understand how the site is used so we can improve it and, with your consent, send news and dates of upcoming programs.
69|Who we share it with
70|Only with those needed for the service: Omnibees (bookings), WhatsApp (conversations), the company that hosts the site and the measurement tools we use. We do not sell personal data.
71|Cookies
72|Cookies store preferences and help measure how the site is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some features may stop working as expected.
73|Your rights
74|Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law 13,709/2018), you may ask us to confirm that we handle your data, and to give you access to it, correct it, delete it, make it portable and revoke your consent, where applicable.
75|How long we keep it
76|For as long as needed to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected and the inn's legal obligations. After that, the data is deleted or anonymized.
77|Changes
78|We may update this policy. The date of the last update is always at the top of this page.
